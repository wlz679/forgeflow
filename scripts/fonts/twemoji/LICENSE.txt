Twemoji graphics vendored into the repository
=============================================

Why these files are here
------------------------
`scripts/build-og-images.ts` renders the 200 per-tool social-preview cards
plus the two brand cards with satori, and substitutes emoji with real PNG
images (satori's bundled opentype.js cannot parse COLR/CBDT colour-emoji
fonts). Those 12 PNGs used to be downloaded from a CDN on every build,
which made the build depend on reaching the network — and a build that
cannot reach the CDN fails outright, so a CDN hiccup would block a
production deploy.

They are vendored here so the build is hermetic. The files are byte-for-byte
the ones the CDN served, so the rendered cards are unchanged.

Which emoji, and what they are for
----------------------------------
The set is closed and asserted by `assertEmojiSet()` in
`scripts/build-og-images.ts`:

  1f3ac  clapper board   the ForgeFlowKit logo mark
  26a0   warning sign    the "act now" family band / tip icon
  2705   check mark      the "healthy" family band
  1f4ca   bar chart      SaaS Metrics category badge
  1f916   robot          AI Cost Tools category badge
  1f48e   gem             Valuation & Exit category badge
  1f4bc   briefcase      Freelance Pricing category badge
  26a1   high voltage   Cost & Efficiency category badge
  1f4c8   chart up       Investment & Real Estate category badge
  1f7e0   orange circle  the "act now" health band
  1f7e1   yellow circle  the "watch" health band
  1f7e2   green circle   the "healthy" health band

Adding an emoji to the set means adding its PNG here too; the build fails
with the exact filename to add if it is missing.
