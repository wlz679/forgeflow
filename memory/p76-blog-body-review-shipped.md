## What was reviewed

100 zh blog bodies generated by sonnet subagent (P75 T1, commit `be300a1`).

### Methodology

1. **Random sampling** — 10 files across multiple categories (ACV, AI training, DSCR, KB coverage, OpenAI Token, etc.)
2. **Systematic issue scan** — grep for known anti-patterns:
   - Spacing between Chinese and English (e.g., `如何使用X` should be `如何使用 X`)
   - English `?` vs Chinese `？` (zh body should use full-width)
   - Calculator name inconsistency
   - Brand name handling
3. **Quality assessment** — overall translation accuracy, structural preservation, tone consistency

## Findings: Translation quality is acceptable

### ✅ Correct patterns observed
- **Spacing**: All `如何使用 X` instances properly spaced (verified via grep — 0 instances of missing space after `使用`)
- **Question marks**: zh body uses Chinese full-width `？` consistently (verified via grep)
- **Calculator names**: Bilingual in parens, e.g., "平均合同金额（ACV）计算器", "DSCR 计算器（偿债覆盖率）", "MRR 计算器"
- **Brand preservation**: "ForgeFlowKit", "MRR", "OpenAI", "Claude", "GPT-5" preserved as-is
- **Structure preservation**: All 5 sections preserved (是什么 / 为什么需要 / 如何使用 / 技巧与最佳实践 / 立即开始)
- **Bullet format**: Markdown `- ` bullets preserved with bold `**...**` patterns
- **Punctuation**: Full-width Chinese `，。：？` used throughout

### 📝 Characteristics (not defects)
- **Template-based repetition**: "无论你是在验证第一个 SaaS 创意..." appears in every file — this is by design (the en body has identical template structure)
- **Calculator-specific descriptions**: First paragraph of each body adapts to the specific tool (AI training costs from 7B to 405B, DSCR NOI/annual debt service, etc.) — translation accurately reflects en descriptions
- **Tech term handling**: "LoRA/全量微调", "GPU 类型（H200、H100、A100、L40S、RTX 6000）" preserved with original mixed EN/CJK — appropriate for technical audience
- **Numerical preservation**: "$10M–$50M ARR", "≥85%", "60-85%" preserved exactly as in en source

### ✅ No fixes required

The translations are competent and ready for user-facing deployment. The mechanical concerns (spacing, punctuation) that often affect AI translations are absent here. Calculator-specific technical content is accurately conveyed.
